Product

ZAPWISE//OS features

Six connected systems that share one design, one action system and one assistant. Each feature is marked honestly: Available now, In beta or Future vision.

Available nowIn betaFuture visionOpen the live demo
ZAP HOME

Your personal dashboard

Time, date, live weather, today's agenda, focus timer, music launcher, apps and quick actions on one screen you can rearrange.

  • Available nowToday card: time, date and live weather (Open-Meteo), hourly and next days
  • Available nowToday's agenda from an imported calendar file (.ics), kept on your device
  • Available nowFocus timer: 15, 25, 50 minutes or your own length, with sessions and minutes today
  • Available nowNow Playing launcher that opens your chosen music app
  • Available nowApps grid in categories, phone dial pad and favorites
  • Available nowCustomize dashboard: show, hide and reorder cards, saved on your device
  • Future visionLive track and playback controls (needs Spotify or another service's sign-in)
  • Future visionHome-screen widgets (need native apps)
ZAP SEARCH

One search for everything

Apps, saved places, notes, actions, websites, shopping and the map from one bar, with the search engine you choose.

  • Available nowOne bar for apps, saved places, notes, actions, websites, shopping and the map
  • Available nowChoose Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing or Brave for web search
  • Available nowAsk ZAP AI straight from search
  • In betaVoice input where your browser supports it
  • Future visionSearch connected files, contacts and calendars (needs your sign-in and permission)
ZAP ACTIONS

Do it, don't just find it

Quick Actions, routines and focus presets. Anything that matters asks you first, and hand-offs say “Opened”, never “Done”.

  • Available nowQuick Actions: Home, Work, Focus, Event, Remind and more, customizable
  • Available nowRoutines: Get me ready for work, Plan my day, Take me home, Find the best headphones
  • Available nowOne action system shared by search, ZAP AI, Quick Actions and the orbit menu
  • Available nowConfirm first: reminders and notes save after a tap; calendar events go through a form and download as a file
  • Available nowHand-offs say “Opened”, never “Done”
  • Future visionMulti-step workflows across connected services
ZAP AI

An assistant that finds the next step

Ask in plain words. Every reply says what it is: an answer, a recommendation, an action to confirm, or a hand-off to another app.

  • Available nowPlain-language commands handled on your device (directions, focus, reminders, weather, conversions)
  • Available nowEvery reply labeled: answer (with its source), recommendation, action to confirm, or needs another app
  • Available nowOnline answers from a free open model, switchable off; bank and portfolio numbers never sent
  • In betaOffline AI brain: a small model that runs in your browser (WebGPU only, about 290 MB once)
  • Future visionSuggestions that use your connected calendar or mail, only with permission
ZAP CONNECT

You control every connection

One screen shows each service, what it can read, what leaves your device, and how to turn it off.

  • Available nowZAP Connect lists every service with live status, what it can read and what leaves your device
  • Available nowControls to refresh, hide, forget or switch off each one (location, weather, traffic, markets, calendar file, online answers, offline brain)
  • In betaBank balances through Plaid, read-only (closed beta)
  • In betaSpending and investment holdings through Plaid (being built)
  • Future visionGoogle Calendar, Spotify, Home Assistant and Apple services, each listed as not connected with what it needs
ZAP LIFE

The wider connected life

Shopping, watching, food, navigation, smart home, TV and car views, built on what today's platforms really allow.

  • Available nowShop: saved list, prices you type in, side-by-side compare, opens the store's own search; no invented prices
  • Available nowWatch: watchlist with a watched toggle; opens streaming services and YouTube
  • Available nowFood: favorite spots; opens maps and delivery apps. ZAPWISE never places orders
  • Available nowNavigate: live map with traffic, saved places, directions
  • Available nowSmart Home: says plainly that no devices are connected, with an optional Home Assistant address
  • Available nowTV view and car view
  • Future visionProduct images and prices from authorized shopping sources
  • Future visionSmart home control, watch apps, CarPlay and Android Auto, AR glasses

Navigation that fits how you like to work

The orbit menu puts ten labeled destinations around the Z: Home, Shop, Watch, Listen, Food, Navigate, Smart Home, Finance, Apps and ZAP AI. Tap, swipe or use the arrow keys. Prefer a plain list? Switch to list view, or just use search.
