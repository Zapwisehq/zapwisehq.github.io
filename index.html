FAQ

Questions

Is ZAPWISE//OS a real operating system?

No. It's a web app that runs in your browser and works like a command center on top of the phone or computer you already have. It doesn't replace iOS, Android, Windows or macOS.

Is it free?

You can open the live demo in your browser today for free. Paid plans are planned only for features that are worth paying for. Nothing for ZAPWISE//OS is for sale today. More on plans.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no ZAPWISE accounts yet; your settings, notes and lists stay in your browser. Accounts and sync between devices are planned.

Can I connect my bank?

Bank balances, spending and linked investments through Plaid are in a closed beta and aren't open to new users yet. It's read-only: ZAPWISE can show your numbers but can't move money.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, in Safari, Chrome and other modern browsers. You can add it to your home screen. Some extras depend on the browser, such as voice input and the offline AI brain. See Get it on every device.

What happens to what I type into ZAP AI?

Commands such as directions, focus and reminders are handled on your device. Open questions go to a free online model only while Online answers is on, and you can switch it off in ZAP Connect. Bank and portfolio numbers are never sent. Details on the Privacy page.

Does Zapwise have a token or coin?

No. Anything claiming to be a Zapwise token is not affiliated with us.
