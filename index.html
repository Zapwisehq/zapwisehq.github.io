How we test

No hype and no made-up numbers. Here's what we do before anything goes out.

01 · Checked first

We check every tool against its maker's own pages (help docs, release notes, official blog) before we make a Short. For brand-new launches we also read trusted news coverage.

02 · Paid? We say so

If something needs a paid plan, is rolling out slowly, is region-limited or has a waitlist, the Short or its page tells you.

03 · Sources linked

Every Short has a page on this site with the tool's official link and the sources we used, so you can check us.

04 · No fake numbers

We never invent stats, reviews or results. Claims from a company are labeled as that company's claim.

05 · Live stats

The numbers in the command center above come straight from YouTube's API and refresh several times a day. The timestamp shows when.
