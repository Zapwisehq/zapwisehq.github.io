Corrections policy

ZAPWISE//OS help

I don't see the newest version

Reload the page once. ZAPWISE//OS checks for updates when it opens and switches to the new version on the next load. On a home-screen icon, close it fully and open it again.

Weather or the map shows the wrong place

Allow location for zapwiseai.com in your browser, or tap the weather card and set a city. With location off, the map says it's showing a default area, not you.

The microphone button is missing

Voice input only appears in browsers that support speech recognition. You can always type instead.

The offline AI brain won't start

It needs a browser with WebGPU and about 1 GB of graphics memory, plus a one-time download of about 290 MB. Many phones can't run it yet; online answers and on-device commands still work.

How do I disconnect a service or delete my data?

Open ☰ Menu → ZAP Connect to switch off or forget each service. To remove everything ZAPWISE saved in your browser, clear this site's data in your browser settings. For a bank link, “Forget bank” in ZAP Connect removes it from this device; email us to remove the link on our server too.

Can I connect my bank?

Not yet. Bank balances through Plaid are in a closed beta. We'll announce on YouTube and X when it opens.
