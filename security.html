Security

Security and trust

Security is part of the product. Here is exactly how ZAPWISE//OS handles your data today, including its limits.

How it's built

  • No secrets in the app. API keys (Plaid, TomTom traffic) live only as secrets in our Cloudflare Workers. The web app never contains them.
  • Locked-down pages. ZAPWISE//OS and our company pages use a Content Security Policy that only allows scripts from this site and network requests to the services listed in ZAP Connect.
  • Rate limits. Our bank and traffic Workers limit requests per address and reject unexpected requests.
  • Static hosting. The site is static files on GitHub Pages over HTTPS. There's no ZAPWISE database of users.

Your data

  • On your device. Notes, reminders, lists, saved places, prices you type, settings and the calendar file you import are saved in your browser's storage.
  • What leaves your device. Only what a feature needs: your location (or city) to the weather and map services, map tiles, and your question to the online AI model while Online answers is on. ZAP Connect lists each one.
  • Bank (closed beta). Read-only through Plaid. You sign in to your bank on Plaid's screen; ZAPWISE never sees your bank password and can't move money. Bank, spending and portfolio numbers are never sent to the AI. Transactions kept on our Worker are encrypted and limited to 90 days.
  • No ads or trackers. No analytics, ad networks or tracking pixels.

You stay in control

  • Every consequential action asks first, and hand-offs to other apps say “Opened”, not “Done”.
  • ZAP Connect lets you switch off or forget each service. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes everything ZAPWISE saved.
  • ZAPWISE never claims an integration works when it doesn't. Unavailable services are labeled “not connected”.

Known limits

  • GitHub Pages can't send custom security headers, so our Content Security Policy is set in each page instead (this can't block other sites from framing a page).
  • Data saved in your browser is only as safe as the device and browser. Use a screen lock, and don't use ZAPWISE on shared computers for personal data.
  • There are no accounts yet, so there's no remote “delete my account”: your data is on your device, and bank links on our server are removed when you email us.

Report a security issue

Email zapwisehq@gmail.com with the subject “Security report”. Please don't test against other people's data or publish details before we've fixed the issue.
